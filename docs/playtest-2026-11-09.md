# Kilometre Zero v0.0.79: play-test report (2026-11-09)

Played as a new player with real mouse and keyboard at 1600x900, then checked at 1920x1080. One full company ("Bellford", 160 minutes of 1x time) and the generated late-game save. No game code changed. Screenshots are in `C:\Users\Aleix\.t3\worktrees\traffic-opus\t3-89c9996a\pt\`.

**The short version.** The first ten minutes are good: two clicks make a working food chain and the town fills in along your road. Then the game stops talking. It took 83 minutes to reach Village (target 20 to 30) because one Food Plant was at its ceiling and every panel said things were fine. Right after Village the steel chain asks for 150 loads of planks from one sawmill, the sawmill gate seizes, and by 160 minutes more than half of all vehicles on the map stood still, both towns had no planks, Bellford was shrinking, and no mine had even finished building. The tools the game tells you to use on a jam (lights, a roundabout) did not help, and the roundabout cut the sawmill off its road. Loading a save deletes every vehicle.

**What I did not finish or could not verify**
- First mine output and first steel: not reached. I stopped at 160 minutes with the sites at 23/30, 25/30 and 43/50 planks.
- I played mostly at speed 3 and converted from the game clock (60 game seconds = 1 real minute at 1x). Waiting times are exact; a real 1x player has more thinking time per game minute.
- First food delivery is a range (12 to 15 min); my recorder went in late.
- Not tested: parallel roads, raised and sunken roads, bus lines, warehouses, convoys, landmarks, special industries, autumn and winter.
- The late-game save is a generated map that is starving (all towns short of food, 34 trucks, $1bn). It showed me the windows and catalog with everything unlocked but no late-game traffic, so my jam analysis comes from my own game.
- Items marked "low confidence" I saw once and did not reproduce.

## A. Blockers and bugs (most damaging first)

**A1. A works gate seizes and takes the whole company with it.**
I placed an iron mine, coal mine, smelter and power plant (all unlocked together at Village). Each is a building site that wants planks (30, 30, 50, 40). My one sawmill became the supplier for four sites and two towns. Its panel went to "28/42 trucks out" while making 1.3 planks an hour. Seven minutes later a TRAFFIC STUCK alert appeared at its gate: 18 vehicles standing, then 44, then 91, then 144 of 270, the longest for 21 minutes. Income fell from $2,917/h to $744/h, the sawmill made 0 planks in an hour, both towns showed "Planks: None!" and Bellford shrank from 314 to 300. I expected a slow road, not a dead one. Shots: [208-stuck.png](C:/Users/Aleix/.t3/worktrees/traffic-opus/t3-89c9996a/pt/208-stuck.png) [221-stuck-panel.png](C:/Users/Aleix/.t3/worktrees/traffic-opus/t3-89c9996a/pt/221-stuck-panel.png) [244-towns-160.png](C:/Users/Aleix/.t3/worktrees/traffic-opus/t3-89c9996a/pt/244-towns-160.png)
Fix: never send more trucks than the gate and road can clear (28 trucks for 1.3 loads an hour is the bug), and give a works a yard to queue in so inbound and outbound trucks do not block each other on the public road.

## F. Ten changes I would make first

1. **Stop the gate gridlock.** Dispatch only what the gate and road can clear; give works an inside queue; test with one sawmill feeding four sites.
2. **Make intersection tools safe around works.** A roundabout or lights on a gate junction keeps the gate joined and gives it a phase.
3. **Save and load the vehicles**, the pause state and the speed.
4. **Name the bottleneck.** Replace "Working normally" and "Enough" at the ceiling with "At full output: build another", and make it the tip.
5. **Shrink and explain building sites.** Small first bill, a visible "13 of 50, about 6 hours", a warning when sites outrun the sawmills.
6. **Fix the catalog.** Pinned tab bar, nothing armed on open, in → out icons on every card.
7. **One Esc, one layer;** closing a window turns its map view off.
8. **Gates face the road by themselves;** rotation resets per building; R and I open Roads and Industry; the wheel always zooms.
9. **Fill minutes 8 to 17.** First goal on the first load, start view framed on the soil, something to do while the first truck drives.
10. **Clear the small lies.** Dead Transport button, "No road yet" on a joined town, the all-towns count under one town's name, cut-off names, truck counts.
