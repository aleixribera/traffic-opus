Scope: the in-play HUD as changed by the interface readout branch (t3/cc1ec054), PC only, at 1600x900, 1920x1080 and 1440x900. Five reviewer lenses gave 52 findings, 42 after merging. Four were dropped as wrong after a check against the game:
- "43 more" and "1,055 a day" are worked out from the same total.
- "At full output" beside Economy's 2 of 12 is two different time windows on a save only six hours old: since its first load, the plant has kept full pace.
- The "long dark line" in the road capture is the town's own outward road.
- The length readout far from the cursor was the capture's "too short" state; the pointer line carries length and cost beside the cursor.

Fixed on the readout branch (v0.0.92): F04, F10, F11, F15, F20 to F23, F33.

Fixed in the second pass, re-captured into after/ and judged in compare.html:
- **F05 / F25, one unit.** Loads a day everywhere goods show: town needs as bars on one shared scale with "0 of 518 loads a day", the works key, the Economy rows (F16 / F17: ratio bars, "4 of 6") and the chart caption. Crates are gone.
- **F01, the goal line.** With a panel open, a GOAL strip above it keeps the goal and its NOW line in view; a click closes the panel. Still open: nothing yet ties the farm's crops to the food the goal asks for.
- **F24 / F29 / F34, the type scale.** Four steps: 12 px uppercase kickers, 14 to 16 px body, 18 to 20 px titles, 24 px headlines. SVG chart axis labels stay at 12 px on purpose.
- **F19.** Demolish sits alone in the panel's footer.
- **F28 / F31, the radio.** A RADIO kicker over the tune in quotes. F28 stays FAIL: the seven top-bar icons still have no visible labels.
- **F27 / F41 / F42.** At far zoom a town's need discs give way to one chip ("Short of 5 needs"); map works badges went from 13 to 16 px.

Still open: F26 (the town's summary sits below the panel's fold), F28's top-bar icons, and the rest marked - in the table.

Not covered: menus, saves and pause (unchanged by this branch), and a long road drawn by the real mouse (checked live instead, pt/c03).

### F01 · After the first farm goes down, the goal and the next step disappear, and 'crops' never connects to the 'food' the goal asks for

- **Where:** farm-no-road @ desktop, box [1291, 11, 298, 218] in [farm-no-road@desktop.png](2026-11-10/captures/farm-no-road%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** ONB-02, Say what the goal is
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time, hud
- **What:** The works panel takes the Goals card's place and the tip goes with it. All that is left is a farm that makes 'crops' and opens in 3 hours, with no 'To' row. The first goal says 'Deliver your first load of food to a town', and the Economy window later lists Crops and Food as separate goods, so a first-timer can't tell whether the farm alone meets the goal or what to build or connect next.
- **Evidence:** Panel: 'Farm Lv 1/4', 'Under construction · 0%', 'Opens in about 3 hours. Its first load of crops follows about 2 hours later.', 'From Fertile soil 100%'. No 'To' row and no goal line on screen. Goal elsewhere: 'Deliver your first load of food to a town'. Economy rows 'Food' and 'Crops' are listed separately. · fresh: Goals card at [1290,10,300,530]. farm-no-road: Farm panel at [1290,10,300,218], with no Goals text in the DOM; the only goal text is 'Goals 0' 'of 20' 'None reached yet' at [21,847].
- **Fix:** Keep a one-line goal strip (NOW/WHERE) visible while a works panel is open, and give the farm panel a 'To' / next-step line that says where its crops go and whether they count as food for Alder Run (e.g. 'Crops go to Alder Run as food: a road links them' or 'Next: a Food Plant turns crops into food').
- **After:** PASS: With a panel open, a GOAL strip sits above it: the goal in full and a NOW line with the next step ('Your Farm: under construction'). Clicking it closes the panel and shows the full Goals card. Still open: nothing yet says the farm's crops become the food the goal asks for

### F04 · The power plant and its coal mine are waiting on each other, and the advice names no way out

- **Where:** late-powerplant @ desktop, box [1300, 68, 280, 170] in [late-powerplant@desktop.png](2026-11-10/captures/late-powerplant%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** NEW
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time, hud
- **What:** NEW: when the cause is a loop, the advice has to name the step that breaks it. The plant waits for coal, and its own advice says the coal mine 290 m away 'works at 40% without full power', which is power this plant would make. The Economy list shows the other fuel (wood chips) blocked as well ('Sawmill · Waiting for logs'). A new player reads three true sentences and still has nothing to build or fix. The headline also seems to flip: the DOM sample from this same capture reads 'Working normally · 40 / 40 MW', while the screenshot shows 'Waiting for coal or wood chips · 0 MW'.
- **Evidence:** Screenshot: 'Waiting for coal or wood chips', 'Your Coal Mine (290 m) sends the next coal: about 4 hours. It works at 40% without full power, so its loads come slowly.', '0 MW made, of 40', '4 buildings in reach, unpowered', '1 /8 coal in the yard'. DOM sample of the same shot: 'Working normally', 'Making 40 / 40 MW'. late-economy: 'Coal Mine · No power', 'Sawmill · Waiting for logs'. · PNG: 'Waiting for coal or wood chips', '0 MW made, of 40', '4 buildings in reach, unpowered', '1 /8 coal in the yard', '0% · then ×2 output'. DOM same capture: 'Working normally' (green 147,201,122), '40 MW made, of 40', '4 buildings powered', '1% · then ×2 output'.

### F05 · Four unexplained units for the same goods: crates, loads a day, made/can-make, more loads a day
