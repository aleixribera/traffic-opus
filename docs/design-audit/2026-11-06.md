38 findings (7 high, 24 medium, 7 low); 29 PASS and 9 FAIL after fixes.

The three that mattered most:
1. **The phone toolbar hid the building tools while the tips sent players to them (F01-F03).** The toolbar now wraps into two rows, and the tool a tip mentions pulses.
2. **The phone overlays menu opened underneath the money and speed bar (F05/F06).** The top-left corner is now stacked above the other corners.
3. **Goal values, primary buttons and the elevation digits failed contrast (F04, F09, F29).** The palette tokens are darker, and the level digits are dark ink.

The FAILs are deliberate or partial:
- **Original style kept:** the small flat type (F15), red "N to go" values (F16) and the purple tip card (F28) match the original game.
- **Touch targets (F07, F20):** they grew to about 30 px, not 44.
- **Satisfaction and flow (F08):** they still show 100% on an empty map.
- **Inspector rows (F35):** unchanged.

Not covered by the captures:
- the bulldoze drag rectangle
- the roundabout and grid previews
- error toasts
- levels 2 and up, with trucks and industry
- safe-area insets on a notched phone (a code-only change)

### F01 · On phone the building tools are off-screen, yet the tips send the player to them

- **Where:** fresh-city @ phone, box [9, 787, 372, 48] in [fresh-city@phone.png](2026-11-06/captures/fresh-city%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** ONB-01, Controls are discoverable in context
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time
- **What:** On a 390 px phone the bottom toolbar shows only the 7 road icons, the grid and half a roundabout. Houses, shop, office, upgrade, bulldoze and undo sit past the right edge, and the only hint is a soft fade. On the next screen the tip says 'Choose a building in the toolbar', but no building is visible, so a new player is stuck at the second step. Same on city, drawing-road, building-panel, signal-panel, saves and level-up phone shots.
- **Evidence:** fresh-city@phone.dom.json lists toolbar controls only up to 'Roundabout (O)' at [360,791,40,40]. 'Junctions', the three building kinds, 'Bulldozer', 'Undo' and 'Redo' are missing from the visible controls. city@phone tip reads 'Place your buildings. Choose a building in the toolbar and click beside a street.'
- **Fix:** On phone, collapse the 7 road types into one road button with a pop-up picker (or two rows), so the building group always shows. At minimum, scroll the toolbar to the building group when the 'Place your buildings' tip appears, and show a clear scroll arrow.
- **After:** PASS: phone toolbar wraps into two rows; buildings group always on screen
