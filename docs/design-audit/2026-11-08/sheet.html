HighF01TYP-01fresh @ desktop

Most secondary text is 11 px

TYP-01: Minimum readable size

Category labels, milestone counts, unlock lines, money per hour, the day, chips and asset subtitles are 11 px; at 1440x900 they are hard to read at a glance.

Evidence: contrast.md: fresh@desktop 18 size fails, all 11 px ('Roads', '0 / 10', '+$0/h', 'Day 1', unlock lines)

Fix: Floor of 12 px for secondary text, 13 px for body; data numerals in a wider tabular face.

Lens: accessibility

MediumF02COL-01fresh @ desktop

Undo/Redo disabled labels at 1.8:1

COL-01: Text contrast over the real background

Disabled Undo and Redo fade to 40% opacity so their labels are 1.79:1; players can't read what the buttons are until they become active.

Evidence: 'Undo' 856,875 contrast 1.79

Fix: Disabled state at ~3:1 (dimmed colour, not opacity), keep label legible.

Lens: accessibility

MediumF03COL-01explore @ desktop

Town label halo is soft; name and size line break up over roads and icons

COL-01: Text contrast over the real background

The town name's text-stroke halo is light cream; over the dark asphalt crossroads and the need icons beneath it, 'VILLAGE · 450' runs into the icons and road markings.

Evidence: contrast.md explore: 'Village · 450' p10 1.15 (shadow), 'Elton' p10 1.43

Fix: Put the town name on a small enamel nameplate (solid dark ground, cream caps) so contrast is fixed regardless of terrain.

Lens: accessibility

MediumF04COL-03explore @ desktop

The UI has no identity: generic white cards and a pale bar read as a web app, not an industry game

COL-03: A small palette with fixed meanings

Every surface is the same near-white card with a 1px grey line and soft shadow. Nothing ties the HUD to the company/industrial theme; the owner wants a 1920s-40s Deco industry skin over TF2's dense layout.

Evidence: --card rgba(255,255,255,.95), --paper #f7f6f1, Lexend everywhere at 11-13 px, blue #1677b3 accent

Fix: Dark enamel-plate panels (navy), cream data text, ochre accent, condensed Deco display face for headings, tabular sans for data.

Lens: hierarchy

MediumF05HUD-04explore @ desktop

Money and date, the numbers a company player acts on, are small and plain

HUD-04: Show the numbers players act on, hide the rest

Money is 13 px bold in the left bar block next to population; the date is 'Day 7' at 11 px. In TF2 the money and date are the anchors of the bottom bar.

Evidence: '$80,225' 13px, 'Day 7' 11px

Fix: Money and clock as stamped numeric readouts (larger tabular digits) in their own plates.

Lens: hud

MediumF06COL-01industry-catalog @ desktop

Locked cards: name and unlock line wash out

COL-01: Text contrast over the real background

Locked catalog cards dim the icon and name to 45% opacity; 'Iron Mine', 'Coal Mine' etc. read at about 2.3:1 so players can't plan what comes next.

Evidence: industry-catalog@desktop contrast fails on locked card names

Fix: Keep locked names at full legibility in a muted colour, mark lock with a stamped tag.

Lens: accessibility

MediumF07COL-03drawing-road @ desktop

The tip pulse on Industry looks exactly like the selected Roads button

COL-03: A small palette with fixed meanings

While Roads is the active category, Industry also shows the selected frame because the hint highlight reuses the same blue border; two categories look active at once.

Evidence: Roads and Industry both framed blue at x 497-630

Fix: Give the hint its own treatment (ochre corner tick / underline pulse), distinct from the selected state.

Lens: hierarchy

MediumF08TYP-02site-panel @ desktop

Panel headings and body text are nearly the same size and weight

TYP-02: A clear type scale with few steps

The site name (13 px/600), section heads (12 px/600) and body (12 px) differ by a pixel, so a player scanning the Farm panel can't find 'Ready to ship' vs 'Last hour' quickly.

Evidence: Farm 13px, 'Ready to ship' 12px 600, 'Crops' 12px 400

Fix: Plate-style title bar (16 px condensed caps) and small-caps section rules distinct from rows.

Lens: hierarchy

LowF09MENU-01main-menu @ desktop

Title card: the game's name is a plain bold web heading

MENU-01: One obvious next step

The title menu is the first impression; the name is a 30px Lexend heading on a white card, no sense of era or company.

Evidence: 'Kilometre Zero' 30px 600 on #f7f6f1

Fix: Enamel nameplate head with a Deco display face and pinstripe rule.

Lens: hierarchy

LowF10MENU-03signal-panel @ desktop

Small targets in the junction editor

MENU-03: Controls are big enough to hit

Arm toggles and duration buttons are 20-22 px tall.

Evidence: contrast.md signal-panel: 11 small targets

Fix: Raise to 26-28 px on desktop.

Lens: accessibility

LowF11FB-01milestone @ desktop

Toasts sit behind the milestone banner

FB-01: Every input answers within a frame or two

A toast fired with a milestone shows at top 56 px, the same place as the banner, so it is hidden.

Evidence: toasts 'Game saved', 'Not enough money' not visible in milestone shot

Fix: Move toasts below the banner zone or above the bottom bar.
