<!-- summary:start -->
Baseline (light web-app UI, before the Deco industry restyle): the three fixes that mattered most were **text size** (F01: most secondary text at 11 px, 18-31 size failures per screen), **identity** (F04: generic white cards that read as a web app, not an industry game) and **legible state** (F02, F06, F07: disabled and locked controls washing out, and the tip pointer looking exactly like a selected tab).

After the restyle (`t3/85849d92`, style system in `docs/ui-style.md`, tokens in `src/ui/theme.css`), all 11 findings pass (compare.html):

- 12 px is the floor: **0 size failures on every screen** at 1440x900 and 1920x1080 (was 2-31 per screen).
- Contrast failures fell from up to 21 per screen to at most 4. The ones left are measurement artefacts: error chips over the red placement preview, town nameplates (the 3-6 px sampling ring lands on terrain just outside the tight plate; pixels inside the label give 13.65:1 and 8.55:1), text right beside a progress bar, and rows clipped at a scroll edge.
- Small targets: junction arms are now 34x32 and panel close buttons 32x32. The 27-29 px tall text buttons are left as they are, which is fine for a mouse-only UI.

Not covered by the captures: the company plate (`hud.setCompany`, checked by hand with a long name at 1440: it truncates, and the explore strip keeps clear of it), and the land/map-tiles tool (reviewed by hand; it is being removed on another branch).

## Details

### F01 · Most secondary text is 11 px

- **Where:** fresh @ desktop, box [1160, 40, 272, 300] in [fresh@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/fresh%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** TYP-01, Minimum readable size
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** Category labels, milestone counts, unlock lines, money per hour, the day, chips and asset subtitles are 11 px; at 1440x900 they are hard to read at a glance.
- **Evidence:** contrast.md: fresh@desktop 18 size fails, all 11 px ('Roads', '0 / 10', '+$0/h', 'Day 1', unlock lines)
- **Fix:** Floor of 12 px for secondary text, 13 px for body; data numerals in a wider tabular face.
- **After:** PASS: 12 px is now the floor everywhere (--t-xs); body 13 px, rows 13-14 px, panel titles 18 px display caps

### F02 · Undo/Redo disabled labels at 1.8:1

- **Where:** fresh @ desktop, box [850, 850, 90, 40] in [fresh@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/fresh%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-01, Text contrast over the real background
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** Disabled Undo and Redo fade to 40% opacity so their labels are 1.79:1; players can't read what the buttons are until they become active.
- **Evidence:** 'Undo' 856,875 contrast 1.79
- **Fix:** Disabled state at ~3:1 (dimmed colour, not opacity), keep label legible.
- **After:** PASS: disabled Undo/Redo keep a dimmed but readable label (#8e8672 on the bar), icons at 55%

### F03 · Town label halo is soft; name and size line break up over roads and icons

- **Where:** explore @ desktop, box [860, 412, 110, 70] in [explore@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/explore%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-01, Text contrast over the real background
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** The town name's text-stroke halo is light cream; over the dark asphalt crossroads and the need icons beneath it, 'VILLAGE · 450' runs into the icons and road markings.
- **Evidence:** contrast.md explore: 'Village · 450' p10 1.15 (shadow), 'Elton' p10 1.43
- **Fix:** Put the town name on a small enamel nameplate (solid dark ground, cream caps) so contrast is fixed regardless of terrain.
- **After:** PASS: town labels are now solid station nameplates (name on a navy plate, size and population on an ochre tab), so terrain no longer shows through the lettering. contrast.mjs still flags them because its 3-6 px sampling ring lands on the terrain just outside the tight plate; sampling inside the label box gives the real ratios

### F04 · The UI has no identity: generic white cards and a pale bar read as a web app, not an industry game

- **Where:** explore @ desktop, box [0, 840, 1440, 60] in [explore@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/explore%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-03, A small palette with fixed meanings
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** hierarchy
- **What:** Every surface is the same near-white card with a 1px grey line and soft shadow. Nothing ties the HUD to the company/industrial theme; the owner wants a 1920s-40s Deco industry skin over TF2's dense layout.
- **Evidence:** --card rgba(255,255,255,.95), --paper #f7f6f1, Lexend everywhere at 11-13 px, blue #1677b3 accent
- **Fix:** Dark enamel-plate panels (navy), cream data text, ochre accent, condensed Deco display face for headings, tabular sans for data.
- **After:** PASS: one Deco industry system (docs/ui-style.md): navy enamel plates with an edge, ochre pinstripe and rivets, display-caps header plates, ochre accent with a single meaning, period icons redrawn light-on-dark

### F05 · Money and date, the numbers a company player acts on, are small and plain

- **Where:** explore @ desktop, box [210, 852, 80, 36] in [explore@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/explore%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** HUD-04, Show the numbers players act on, hide the rest
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** hud
- **What:** Money is 13 px bold in the left bar block next to population; the date is 'Day 7' at 11 px. In TF2 the money and date are the anchors of the bottom bar.
- **Evidence:** '$80,225' 13px, 'Day 7' 11px
- **Fix:** Money and clock as stamped numeric readouts (larger tabular digits) in their own plates.
- **After:** PASS: money and clock sit in inset ink readouts, 18 px Barlow 600 tabular figures; income line below in green/red; day in caps

### F06 · Locked cards: name and unlock line wash out

- **Where:** industry-catalog @ desktop, box [500, 724, 546, 96] in [industry-catalog@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/industry-catalog%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-01, Text contrast over the real background
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** Locked catalog cards dim the icon and name to 45% opacity; 'Iron Mine', 'Coal Mine' etc. read at about 2.3:1 so players can't plan what comes next.
- **Evidence:** industry-catalog@desktop contrast fails on locked card names
- **Fix:** Keep locked names at full legibility in a muted colour, mark lock with a stamped tag.
- **After:** PASS: locked cards keep a dashed border and dimmed icon but the name and unlock line stay legible

### F07 · The tip pulse on Industry looks exactly like the selected Roads button

- **Where:** drawing-road @ desktop, box [496, 844, 136, 54] in [drawing-road@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/drawing-road%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-03, A small palette with fixed meanings
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** hierarchy
- **What:** While Roads is the active category, Industry also shows the selected frame because the hint highlight reuses the same blue border; two categories look active at once.
- **Evidence:** Roads and Industry both framed blue at x 497-630
- **Fix:** Give the hint its own treatment (ochre corner tick / underline pulse), distinct from the selected state.
- **After:** PASS: the tip's pointer is now ochre lettering plus a pulsing ochre diamond in the corner; the selected tab is a raised plate-3 with an ochre top bar and caret. They no longer share a look

### F08 · Panel headings and body text are nearly the same size and weight

- **Where:** site-panel @ desktop, box [1160, 8, 272, 436] in [site-panel@desktop.png](2026-11-08/captures/site-panel%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** TYP-02, A clear type scale with few steps
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** hierarchy
- **What:** The site name (13 px/600), section heads (12 px/600) and body (12 px) differ by a pixel, so a player scanning the Farm panel can't find 'Ready to ship' vs 'Last hour' quickly.
- **Evidence:** Farm 13px, 'Ready to ship' 12px 600, 'Crops' 12px 400
- **Fix:** Plate-style title bar (16 px condensed caps) and small-caps section rules distinct from rows.
- **After:** PASS: panel titles are 18 px display caps on a header plate with an ochre rule; section heads 12 px ochre caps with a hairline; body 13-14 px
