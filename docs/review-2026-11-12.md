# Kilometre Zero v0.0.108: review against Cities: Skylines and Transport Fever 2 (2026-11-12)

I played this as a fresh, demanding reviewer. I used a real mouse and keyboard at 1600x900, in a headless, muted browser on the same RTX 5060 (ANGLE/D3D11) as the last review.

**What I played**
- A new company, "Tri-State Motor Freight", for about 34 real minutes at speeds 1 to 4. I took it from an empty map to First steel, Two villages and a third town.
- The generated late-game save (`tools/out/lategame.json`, built on 2026-11-10), for about 20 minutes:
  - 3 towns and 5,300 people;
  - 47 to 182 vehicles;
  - the road tool played hard on it: raised roads, bridges, roundabouts, curves, undo and redo.
- No game code was changed.

**How the scores work.** As last time, each category is scored against `docs/direction.md`:
- It is a company game, with no land and no trucks to buy.
- Difficulty is traffic, not money.
- Towns grow in bands.
- Lights run on fixed timers.
- It is a PC game.
- The camera is a tilted Diorama, with a straight-down Map view.
- All sound is synthesized.

CS means Cities: Skylines (1 and 2), and TF2 means Transport Fever 2. Screenshots are local only, in `.scratch/review2/` (not committed). File names are given in brackets.

**Compared with v0.0.99 (review of 2026-11-10), this is again a clearly better game.** Nearly every bug from the last review is fixed, the first hour is twice as fast, and the late game holds 144 fps with half the triangles.

| | v0.0.99 | v0.0.108 |
|---|---|---|
| First food landed | about 7.5 min | about 2.5 min |
| Village | about 23 min | about 10.7 min |
| First steel | about 49 min | about 30 min |
| Two villages | about 49 min | about 27 min |
| Late save, standing still | 3 to 14 places, never cleared | 0 every time I looked (lighter load, see category 4) |
| Late save, triangles | 6.5 to 7.5 M | 3.2 to 4.4 M |
| Late save, worst frame in 45 s at speed 4 | 63 to 70 ms, 2 or 3 every 15 s | one 97 ms frame, right after a zoom change |

The weak spots have moved again:
- **Advice that changes its mind.** The game's words were its best feature; now its forecasts swing hour to hour, and it often says the same fact three times.
- **The road tool at the edges**: crossing a river right behind a bank road, and a Tool Options panel that moves under the pointer.
- **Traffic at scale is unproven.** The late save no longer carries the load that deadlocked v0.0.99, because its towns are shrinking for lack of planks.

**What I could not verify**
- **Sound.** The browser was muted, as the owner asked.
- **Edge scroll.** It only runs while `document.hasFocus()` is true (`src/app.ts:2394`), and a headless page never has focus.
- **Frame-rate headroom.** The run is capped at 144 fps by vsync.
- **Not tested:** buses, convoys, landmarks, special industries and parallel roads.

### 1. Visual look and art direction: 7.5 (was 7.0)

**CS and TF2.** CS2 has a realistic town at street level and a dense city from above. TF2 has a strong period look and terrain with real relief. Both fill the screen with detail at every zoom.

**This game.**
- **Winter is fixed (B3).** Snow now lies on lots, parks and fields; houses and fenced plots read cleanly through it, and the snowfall is visible over the river [131-winter].
- **The hall square is furnished**: market stalls with striped awnings, benches, trees and paths. A few pedestrians are visible at close zoom [078-square].
- **Night.**
  - Lamp pools light the streets and the square [079-steelwait].
  - The night Map view is readable: lit street dots over the atlas [140-quickload].
- **Weather** adds rain streaks and snowfall [130-rain-night, 131-winter].
- The atlas map at far zoom is still the best picture in the game [069-camp2, 101-late-map].

**Evidence against a 9**
- **Works yards are still mostly bare dirt pads at level 1**, and a level-1 farm is bare fields.
- **Spring opens grey.** Mist and showers wash out the first minutes of a new game. This is by design (`MIST_CHANCE` 0.22 in spring, `src/game/weather.ts`), but it is the player's first impression.
- **Two odd marks:**
  - forest canopies in spring are covered in white dots (blossom) that read as noise at mid zoom;
  - pale-green disc "floodlight pools" sit in the logging camp yard by day.
- **The Village Hall stands on a large plot outlined in white** [078-square]. It reads like a selection box.
- **Night lighting was not dependable.** In the late save the scene looked fully lit at 00:14 and 02:29 [124-undo, 107-boul], while a later run of the same save was dark at 04:19 [140-quickload]. This is low confidence; I could not find a cause, and `__env.set({hour})` did not change the light either [130-rain-night].

**To reach a 9**
1. Give level-1 yards stock piles and parked trucks, and sow level-1 fields.
2. Start a new game in fair weather: no mist or rain in the first game days.
3. Tone the blossom dots down to a tint, and hide yard floodlight pools by day.
4. Drop the white plot outline around halls.

**CS and TF2.** Both have smooth, zoom-scaled WASD and edge-scroll, a reliable wheel zoom, orbit, and a follow camera.

**This game.**

2. Show Q/E and Y/H in the Camera window as buttons, not only as text.

### 3. Road building: 7.5 (was 7.0)

**CS and TF2.** CS has straight, curve and freeform modes, upgrades, elevation and guidelines. TF2 has the cleanest long-distance tool, with automatic bridges and slope readouts.

**This game.**
- **The catalog folds to a one-line strip while you draw**, and has a HIDE control [126-curve].
- **Bridges are automatic and priced:** "Bridge 136 m over water · $16,922 for the bridge · Steepest climb 10.7%" [115-ground].
- **Curves are smooth and chain** from the last end point [127-curve2].
- **Undo and redo work for roads and roundabouts** [124-undo, 128-undo2, 129-redo].
- **Roundabouts:**
  - The hover preview names the cost: "Demolishes 4 buildings, $600 compensation · $2,370".
  - A second click confirms: "Click again to demolish 4 buildings".
  - The ring comes out clean, with a planted centre [119-rb-hover, 122-rb-click, 123-rb-built].
- **Far works get an offered road.** Placing a works far from a road gets a dashed "dirt road offered" ghost and a one-click "Build this road · $716" button in its panel.

**Evidence against a 9**
- **A river crossing right behind a bank road could not be built in six attempts.** Each fix ran into a different refusal, and the refusals sent me in a circle [107-boul to 117-ground-up2]. The attempts:
  1. Ground, starting on the bank road: "Start 41 m further back to ramp up over the water".
  2. Raised to 18 m, starting on open ground: "Start or end a raised road on a road".
  3. Raised, starting on the bank road: the same refusal.
  4. Ground start on the bank road, end raised to 18 m: "Start 25 m further back to ramp up over the water".
  5. Ground, starting behind the bank road and crossing it: "Only 1.6 of 4.6 m clearance, raise or lower it with Page Up or Down".
  6. The same draft, raised with Page Up: "Start or end a raised road on a road", and the ghost and price vanish.

  In TF2 the new road would simply cross the bank road at grade and ramp up after it. A town on a river is the commonest place to want a second bridge.
- **The Tool Options panel moves under the pointer.**
  - It is anchored to the bottom (`.toolopts`, `src/ui/hud.css:256`) and grows upward as its readout gains lines (length, cost, climb, bridge, angle).
  - So the mode and elevation buttons jump up and down by up to 200 px between hovers [023-road2, 024-road3, 108-raised, 114-raised4].
  - My click on "+" for elevation landed on empty panel.
  - When it is tall it slides under the Traffic window, hiding its own title [108-raised].
- **Refusal chips appear in different places.** Most sit by the cursor; the clearance refusal appeared at the top right, over the goals header [108-raised, 115-ground].

**To reach a 9**
1. **Let a ground road cross another road at grade before its ramp.** Join the bank road as a junction, then start the ramp after it. The same applies to a raised road starting on land: give it a ramp from ground level.
2. **Pin the Tool Options panel's top edge,** and put the growing readout below the controls.
3. **Clamp every refusal chip to the cursor.**

### 4. Traffic simulation and feel: 7.0 (was 5.5)

**CS and TF2.** In both, jams build and clear once you fix them; nothing locks up for good.

**This game.**
- **The late save now flows.** Across about 20 minutes at speed 4, the Traffic window read "Standing still 0 of N" every time I looked, from N = 47 at night to N = 182 in the morning [102-traffic, 108-raised, 112-raised2].
- **The new game at 96 vehicles** never stalled.
- Per-car motion is smooth.
- Trucks and cars mix well on the bridge.
- The day/night swing is mild, as the direction asks: 47 vehicles at night, about 180 at the morning peak.

**Evidence against a 9**
- **The deadlock fix (B1) has not been seen under the old load.**
  - The late save peaks at 182 vehicles, against 120 to 250 in v0.0.99.
  - Its towns shrink from the moment it loads ("Shrinking: 0% supplied"), because the builder lays no Sawmill and "Nothing of yours makes planks" [100-late].
  - Alder Run fell from 2,154 to 1,924 people during my session.
- **The Poplar Creek junction cluster** that locked in v0.0.99 sits in town roads laid by the older code. I did not see the new "Deadlock" stuck panel at all, so I cannot say how it reads.
- **No player-made jam.** In 34 minutes of a new game I never caused a jam worth solving. Since traffic is the difficulty, the first jam should arrive inside the first hour.

**To reach a 9**
1. **Make the late save a real load test** (see the top 5, item 5): supplied towns, about 250 vehicles, and the old Poplar Creek cluster. Then confirm "Standing still" stays near 0 for 10 game days.
2. **Make sure the first hour produces one readable jam** at a works gate or a town junction, with the stuck panel explaining it.

### 5. Freight and industry depth: 7.5 (was 7.0)

**CS and TF2.** TF2's chains are deep and visible. Neither explains a stalled chain as well as this game tries to.

**This game.**
- **B2 is fixed by the offered road.** A logging camp 650 m out, a coal mine 2.2 km out and a second farm 500 m out each got a dashed road and a one-click build.
- **B4 is gone,** and **B5 is fixed:** the card says "Burns coal or wood chips".
- **Shortfall lines are concrete:** "Food: 35 more loads a day. Your Food Plant is at full output. Build about 4 more." [104-late-wide].
- **The site panel names the bottleneck:** "Your Smelter: waiting for iron ore. A load of iron ore is on the way from your Iron Mine 3.7 km away: about 3 hours. It works at 40% without full power, so its loads come slowly." [079-steelwait].

**Evidence against a 9**
- **Site forecasts swing by an order of magnitude.** The Smelter's building site read, in order:
  1. "about 2 days to go" (3 of 24);
  2. "about 3 days";
  3. "about 4 days";
  4. "about 11 days" [074-saw-905_665];
  5. then "about 13 hours" (16 of 24), then "about 7 hours" (22 of 24) [076-town-close, 077-street-night].

  The site was finished about one game day after the "11 days" reading. The ETA divides the loads left by the average of the last 12 hourly deliveries (`Industry.siteEta`, `src/game/industry.ts:1635`, `SITE_HOURS = 12`). Planks arrive in bursts, so the rate swings. The "At this pace that is days: another Sawmill would bring planks faster" advice (`src/ui/inspect.ts:332`) fires on the noise. It pushed me to build a second Sawmill, and the site jumped from 3 to 16 of 24 before that Sawmill could have opened, so the old one was enough.
- **Nothing says where the planks went.**
  - While the site sat at 3 of 24, the company had made 85 planks and sold 39 to towns.
  - The site panel never says "your towns are taking the planks first"; it only says "another Sawmill".
- **The food advice flips within a game day** [065-farm2]:
  - first "Food Plant is short of crops… Your Farm is at full output. Build another";
  - then "Your Food Plant is at full output… Build another Food Plant, or wait for level 3".
- **The offered road is missing in two places**, with no reason given:
  - the Smelter about 200 m from a road ("No road in reach of its gate");
  - the iron deposit 3.8 km out [052-smghost3, 058-smsel].

**To reach a 9**
1. **Forecast a site from supply, not from recent deliveries:** producer output times the share the dispatcher gives the site. Round it to bands ("about a day", "2 to 3 days"), and only change band after it has held for a few hours.
2. **When towns are outbidding a site, say so** ("Your towns take 4 of every 5 planks"), and offer the fix that is really there.
3. **Hold advice for a few game hours** before it can flip to its opposite.
4. **Offer a road to every works that has none,** or say why not.

## 2. (a) The single weakest point: advice that changes its mind

The game's distinctive strength is that it tells you, in plain sentences, what is happening and what to do. In v0.0.108 that voice is the least reliable part of the game:

- **Forecasts swing by an order of magnitude.**
  - The Smelter site read 2, 3, 4, then 11 days, then 13 hours and 7 hours, and was finished about one game day after "11 days".
  - The cause is `Industry.siteEta` (`src/game/industry.ts:1635`): it divides the loads left by a 12-hour average of bursty deliveries.
  - Its "At this pace that is days: another Sawmill…" advice (`src/ui/inspect.ts:332`) told me to spend $16,160 on a second Sawmill; the site then jumped from 3 to 16 of 24 before that Sawmill could have opened [074-saw-905_665, 075-saw2placed, 077-street-night].
- **Advice flips.** The food chain went from "Farm at full output, build another Farm" to "Food Plant at full output, build another Food Plant" within a game day [065-farm2].
- **It hides the real cause.** The site sat at 3 of 24 planks while 39 planks went to towns, and no line says so.
- **It repeats itself.**
  - New town: card, tip and pointer [080-next].
  - "No road reaches its gate", twice in one panel [058-smsel].
- **A price is misattributed.** "Road offered" sits beside the works' price [011-campghost, 039-coaldep].

Everything else that scored low (traffic proof, empty blocks, sound) is either untested or a matter of more content. This is the one place where the game says something false to the player at the moment they act on it, during its most important stretch: the 11 to 30 minute climb to First steel.

## 2. (b) Top 5 changes, ranked by score gained per unit of work

**1. Forecast sites from supply, and hold advice steady** (Freight +0.5, First hour +0.25, Progression +0.25, Identity +0.25).
- In `Industry.siteEta` (`src/game/industry.ts:1635`), replace the 12-hour delivered average with expected supply: the hourly output of each producer that serves the site, times the share of its loads the dispatcher sends there. Keep the delivered average only as a floor once a site has 6 hours of history.
- In `siteStatus` and `siteHours` (`src/ui/bottleneck.ts:747`), round to bands: "a few hours", "about a day", "2 to 3 days". Change band only after the new band has held for 3 game hours. Store the band on the site.
